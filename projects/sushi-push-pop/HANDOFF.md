## The idea in one paragraph
Brand: Yoko Ono Asian Kitchen. The Sushi Push Pop is a black tube with a gold logo, a soy vial clipped to the side, and a clear lid; you push the roll up from the bottom.
The hero is the brand kid (anime kid from the label and sticker). He walks in a locked side profile while the world changes behind him (snow → desert → sea → jungle). Transitions are object wipes: a snow wall, a dune crest, wave spray, leaves.
He arrives once, at a cliff over the jungle, and pulls out the push pop. The product shot is locked dead centre while the worlds whip past behind it. Then an ASMR macro: vial click → lid pop → soy pour → push-up *tik-tik-tik* → bite → end card.
The hook (first 1s) is the push-up + POP. Format: 9:16, 15s.
