# Sushi Push Pop: "Anywhere" (v2, 2026-11-17)

**Brand:** Yoko Ono Asian Kitchen. The brand kit is in `brand/yoko-ono/` (logo, product, how-to, brand kid).
**Hero:** the **brand kid** from the label and sticker (`brand/yoko-ono/character/kid_eating_pushpop.png`), black tee, messy dark hair.
**Message:** sushi that goes with you anywhere.
**Format:** 9:16, about 15s at 24fps, plus a 6s cutdown (product + ASMR only).

## Structure (user notes, v2)
- Walk in **locked side profile**, the world changes behind him. Transitions are **object wipes**: a snowdrift, wave spray, leaves.
- **One arrival only:** a summit or a cliff over the jungle. He sits and pulls out the push pop.
- **Product = the star.** Long, satisfying ASMR beats that follow the real 4-step use.

## Sound = the selling point
Every product beat is ASMR, close-mic and dry. Music drops out at shot 7, so only the product sounds remain:
- the vial click,
- the lid pop,
- the soy glug,
- the push ticks,
- the bite crunch.

Music comes back on the logo. Foley is built in post: free local synthesis plus layering.

## Production approach
- Character consistency comes from one reference sheet of the brand kid. It goes into every shot as a reference image.
- Product shots (6–9) use the real label and how-to renders as references. The tube must match `brand/yoko-ono/product/`.
- Each clip is generated at 4–5s, and only the best 1.2–2s is used.
