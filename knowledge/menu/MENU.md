## What the menu tells us
- **The menu's own look:** "סטריט סושי בר" (Street Sushi Bar). White paper, orange-red type, a red rule at the edge, and hand-drawn line illustrations of street stalls, bowls and bottles.
  - This is a different look from the Noir & Or of the dice video and the screen mockups. Decide which one the screen follows.
- **Section names:**
  - סטריט סושי בר (Street Sushi Bar)
  - סטריט סושי פארטי (Street Sushi Party)
  - ווק פארטי (Wok Party)
  - קומבינציות (Combinations)
- **Kosher:**
  - Shrimp is replaced by זהבנון fish, called "kosher shrimp substitute".
  - Crab is surimi.
  - Cream and cheese are vegan.
- **Special rolls:** 8 pieces, and changes are not allowed. A soy wrap costs +7.
- **Classic rolls:** 8 pieces, a soy wrap is +7, and a **push-pop version is +12**.
- **Spicy wok:** +8.
- **Mango:** "*בהתאם לעונה" (depends on the season), and can be swapped for another seasonal fruit.
- **Not on this menu:** desserts, cocktails and alcohol. The 2024 shoot has mochi, desserts and cocktails, so ask whether there's a separate drinks and desserts menu.
- **In the 2024 shoot but not on this menu:** pad kapao, tonkatsu, bœuf caramélisé, darne saumon, poisson du jour, yakitori saumon, curry poulet, curry and tofu noodles, chicken lemon. Don't put these on the screen without asking.
